Instructions about the fest

Here are some important instructions for the fest:

  • Please arrive on time for all events.
  • Wear the official fest t-shirt for all activities.
  • Follow the code of conduct at all times.
  • Only one guest is allowed per ID.
  • No entry without a valid pass.

Directions to reach IIMB:

  1. Arrive at Majestic Metro station.
    • Be careful of pickpockets.
    • The metro station closes at 10 PM.
  2. Take the metro to JP Nagar.
  3. From JP Nagar, take a taxi or auto to IIMB.
